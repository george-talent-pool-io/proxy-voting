# Reliable classifier and calibrated learning framework — evaluation

22 October 2026. AI-authored framework qualification on the development Mac; **not customer accuracy and not an automation certificate**.

## Decision

The balanced specialist learned substantially better, and the training/release framework caught and now prevents a real immutable-checkpoint violation by the official SDK. However, the requested dependable low-review autonomous classifier has **not** been established. The confidence operating-point gate failed. Final-decision quality is materially lower than semantic-field quality because evidence guards introduce conflicts. A production-ready classifier or small customer review rate cannot honestly be claimed.

The current framework implements the customer learning/qualification workflow and remains review-only until each deployment passes its own independent gate. Numerical calibration below is for specialist-only outputs in the declared synthetic population. It is **not** numerical calibration of an actual Laya–LLM hybrid. The application has a separate hybrid calibration workflow, but this study did not establish a supported hybrid operating point.

## Qwen 4 verification

No official downloadable Qwen 4 weights were found in the live official Hugging Face catalogue. The Qwen4-27B NVFP4 repository is a preparation repository without weights; the official Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is an architecture preview, not a locally runnable Qwen4-27B release. No substitute was called Qwen 4 and no additional model was downloaded. See [the primary-source research](MODEL_AVAILABILITY_QWEN4.md). Existing local Qwen3.8-27B MLX was exercised only as the clearly identified real application integration reference. Historical 92/95 Qwen results came from a different serving condition and were not reproduced or transferred here.

## Architecture and product boundary

Frozen foundation → customer TRAIN → VALIDATION selection → freeze weights/criteria/preprocessing/rules → separate specialist-routing and final-output calibration → independent frozen qualification → explicit promotion → monitored processing → low-confidence review plus random accepted-decision audits → a new learning round.

Normalise/detect input. Genuine SWIFT uses Prowide; partial messages use lenient recovery. Ordinary notices enter the semantic path directly. Deterministic code handles dates/state; Laya supplies factorised semantic facts; a local LLM may resolve escalations; reconciliation preserves disagreements. Final confidence is separately fitted for the resulting architecture and population. The parser was not expanded in this phase.

AI qualification corpora, research generators, learned test checkpoints, private calibrators and databases remain outside the shipped source/image. The frozen selected AI derivative is `reliable-voting-balanced-v1-selected`, identity `25f82045bc885bed5e6c1303df6ac09fde78346679668e39674fcdaa7478a756`; it is not a customer-qualified foundation and is not bundled.

## Training and software causes

The preceding 160-notice adaptation sample had 138 VOTABLE, 11 NOT_VOTABLE and 11 UNCERTAIN examples. Votability also supplied only one of nine training questions. The new TRAIN role has 319 VOTABLE, 319 NOT_VOTABLE and 198 UNCERTAIN records, with short, medium and long notices across classes. It has 624 unique texts. Votability is weighted fourfold using TRAIN-only official-SDK question-item repetition; this changes task exposure without inflating independent-example counts.

Official Laya SDK 0.4.8; foundation lineage `convaiinnovations/laya:multilingual` revision `7b928d828b7b0e022f929d9bd2e44165aa270148`; seed 42; max context 1,024; no internal model-confidence calibration or training on dates. The original two-epoch full-encoder run was interrupted under resource pressure after retaining one completed encoder epoch (627 updates). The partially completed second epoch was discarded. A distinct lower-memory head adaptation retained another 627 updates, learning rate 1e-5, micro-batch 16. Its successful registered execution took approximately 211 seconds. No two-full-encoder-epoch result is claimed.

A failed intermediate job correctly detected the SDK rewriting `tokenizer/tokenizer_config.json` in the frozen base. The rewrite changed compatibility metadata, not weights. It was not silently accepted or relabelled completed. Training and serving now give the SDK private copies, never hard links, and the original integrity guard remains. Actual-SDK tests verify the rewrite cannot touch the source. The successful isolated job has zero native-score difference across two fresh checkpoint loads. Recovery, the failed job and the interrupted job remain separately recorded.

## End-to-end evidence gap

93 test notices had every semantic field correct but an incorrect final status. Of the 114 final-status errors, 112 carried a scoped-evidence conflict warning. This is a measured association, not proof that all 112 warnings are wrong. The conservative evidence locator can interpret a negative/irrelevant passage as contradictory and forces review. Training accuracy alone does not solve this. No guard was removed or weakened to improve a headline result. An evidence-routing comparison must be selected on validation, preserve true negation/supersession/conflict protections, and then use a fresh independent test.

## Calibration and operating gate

Correctness calibration uses separate LAYA_STAGE (726) and specialist FINAL (839) partitions, each with group-isolated FIT/EVALUATION folds. The FINAL partition has 555 FIT records / 390 groups and 284 EVALUATION records / 215 groups. Seven heads use a Laplace-smoothed constant because fewer than ten FIT mistakes prevent meaningful binary discrimination; three use fixed L2 logistic calibration. Constant population rates are not personalised confidence ranking. Model self-assessment and native Laya probabilities are not labelled calibrated probabilities.

Version `customer-correctness-v4` isolates source/engine, class, evidence mode, language, input type and supersession strata. Final calibration also separates LLM-invoked from specialist-only paths. Unseen/unsupported/OOD conditions return null and review. Decision trust is fitted separately; the minimum of field values is only an operational all-fields trust gate.

## Actual local performance

Apple M3 Max, 64-GB unified-memory development Mac. Warm per-notice median **103.0 ms**, mean **101.6 ms**, p95 **155.3 ms**, minimum **61.0 ms**, maximum **205.3 ms** over 425 actual selected-Laya requests. Model initialisation 3.78 seconds; total rate including load and durable writes 7.62 notices/sec. These are whole-notice specialist timings, not hybrid/LLM or EC2 measurements.

After-notice samples reached approximately 2.13 GB process RSS and 2.77 GB MPS driver allocation. They are not OS-wide peak unified-memory measurements and must not be added as independent physical memory. The host was a general-purpose desktop; training experienced swap pressure. Actual Linux local-model training/inference on the intended EC2/Fargate configuration remains unqualified.

## Framework changes and verification

- TRAIN-only task weighting with honest unique-record and effective-question counts.
- Private SDK working/model copies, source-integrity checks and load/save parity.
- Engine-specific calibration strata and no transfer from specialist calibration to LLM results.
- Verified full-source negative evidence basis for NOT_VOTABLE; never an invented quote.
- Versioned random audits of otherwise accepted decisions (default 2%, permitted 1–50%), independent of confidence; inclusion receipts persist across restart.
- Verified audited errors suspend the qualification; AI labels cannot impersonate expert reviews.
- Original evaluation time retained and prefilled for review; elapsed deadlines do not become false model-error suspensions.
- Qualification review-rate budget includes the expected random-audit load.
- Individual customer training-stage time budgets, process-group cancellation and preserved failures.
- Typed default-only MLX serving-request compatibility; genuine setting/checkpoint changes still fail closed.
- Semantic decision uncertainty separated from operational release permission. Clear facts do not grant automation; the customer worker continues to require a passed deployment qualification. No synthetic-origin claim is hard-coded into real customer decisions.

Current-source verification: **2,289 ordinary tests passed, one skipped and 168 opt-in tests deselected; 34 browser tests passed; three actual local Laya/Qwen customer-worker tests passed in 64.59 seconds**. The real tests invoke both neural models and validate persisted decisions, source-exact evidence, native distributions, confidence refusal and audit records. A separate four-event real-model campaign also completed. Browser tests cover two learning rounds, protected labels, qualification refusal, confidence display, audit/restart and review. Linux service verification checks named authentication, non-root/read-only execution, startup and restart persistence; it explicitly does **not** execute Linux models. `DELIVERY_VERIFICATION.json` records exact current source hashes and post-study changes.

The frozen test transition matrix contains **555 repaired assertions and 29 damaged assertions** (net +526). Critical gains must be read alongside those damages; improvement is not universal. The exact per-field matrix is retained.

Two software fixture setups were extended to include the now-required private-copy source directory. The old universal semantic-review assertions in the semantic/MLX unit tests and a duplicate real-test assertion were intentionally replaced: semantic uncertainty and operational permission now have separate owners. The real customer-worker test still asserts persisted review is required and automation is disabled. No expected challenge answer was changed.

## Remaining gates and next experiment

**COMPLETE ENGINEERING:** versioned local training, immutable parents, validation selection, registered model loading, protected splits, calibration machinery, qualified-release controls, random audits, review revisions, persistence and source-delivery exclusion.

**NOT ESTABLISHED:** reliable low-review specialist operating point; numerical confidence for an actual selected Laya–LLM hybrid; independent linguistic-family generalisation; a downloadable Qwen 4 benchmark; Linux/GPU model deployment qualification; representative expert-labelled customer accuracy. The new private AI model is experimental, not a promised production foundation.

The highest-value next experiment is an evidence-routing ablation on development validation: compare the scoped lexical locator with native sentence/passage support while preserving genuine conflict and supersession review. Separately clarify the scope of OTHER_RESTRICTION, which currently over-fires. Freeze the chosen inference/criteria configuration, generate a genuinely new family-held-out test, and calibrate the actual registered specialist and resulting hybrid independently. Do not tune this change on the 57 cases or re-use the opened 425 examples as a fresh independent test. More epochs or another opaque layer should follow only if that experiment identifies a model-learning bottleneck.

A commercial deployment still needs its own representative corpus, independent expert qualification, supported model/runtime/hardware and explicit promotion. `AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` remains the shipped default. This report does not certify a production-ready autonomous voting service.
