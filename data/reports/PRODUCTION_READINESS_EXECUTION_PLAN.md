# Production readiness execution plan

22 October 2026. This programme qualifies the learning framework and its declared
AI-authored test population. It cannot certify accuracy on an unseen customer
population. Automation remains disabled until that deployment passes its gate.

## Preserved baseline

The committed reliability study, its checkpoints, manifests, 425 opened synthetic
test records and unchanged 57-case challenge remain immutable references. The
opened test is now diagnostic, not a fresh qualification set. No challenge answer
may select inference rules, thresholds, prompts or training examples.

## Execution order and release gates

1. **Evidence correctness.** Compare whole-notice scoped candidates against
   native passage verification on VALIDATION only. Preserve document labels,
   complete native passage distributions, exact quotations and warnings. Genuine
   contradictions, uncertain voting routes and unresolved supersession require
   review. A locator must not interpret unrelated prose as contrary evidence.
   Unit minimal pairs and actual-model validation must pass before selection.
2. **Freeze the inference condition.** Pin the selected checkpoint, inference
   implementation, decision criteria, preprocessing and code hashes. Selection
   uses validation final-status quality, semantic quality, critical misses and
   latency. Retain the losing variants and their full predictions.
3. **Independent qualification material.** Construct new fact-first, provenance-
   labelled notices with separately authored linguistic families, role/group
   isolation and protected labels. Include current/historical/superseded terms,
   unrelated negatives, conditional routes and unresolved contradictions. Record
   exact/near-duplicate checks and disclose AI authorship; never call it expert
   validation. Freeze inputs and keys before scoring.
4. **Calibrate the actual execution paths.** Fit/evaluate specialist routing and
   resulting hybrid output confidence separately on group-isolated calibration
   folds. No specialist calibration may authorise an LLM output. Preserve null
   confidence for unsupported populations, repaired schemas and unsafe evidence.
   Select an operating point on VALIDATION only; independently report support,
   Brier/ECE, critical recall, review load and precision lower bounds.
5. **Deployable execution.** Exercise registered models through the durable
   customer worker, with source seals, restart persistence, audit/review and
   promotion refusal. Verify the Linux service separately from neural-runtime
   qualification. Record unsupported hardware/runtime conditions explicitly.
6. **Delivery verification.** Run unit/integration, browser and opt-in real-model
   suites; build and smoke-test the Linux image; verify source delivery excludes
   private corpora, test weights, keys and calibrators. Publish exact test counts,
   benchmark denominators, artifacts and all remaining failed gates.

## Acceptance criteria

- No lost source/evidence/history; displayed quotations are exact substrings.
- No calibration or model-selection leakage; no mutable frozen checkpoints.
- Real neural inference, training/reload, calibration and qualification paths are
  executable and audited, rather than satisfied only by test doubles.
- Numerical confidence is population/model/path-specific and support-checked.
- A passed automation gate requires independent sample support and conservative
  critical-positive/precision bounds; a small review percentage is measured,
  never manufactured by relaxing the gate.
- Random audits of accepted decisions feed future learning rounds; verified
  errors can suspend a release. Customer-specific qualification remains required.

## Development amendment: match evidence training to serving

The original validation compositions improved from 290/374 to 363/374 final
statuses after evidence fixes, but the newly authored validation family exposed
poor transfer: 1276/1440 fields and 51/160 final statuses. Native sentence
verification reused document-trained heads. This is an input-distribution
mismatch, so the next experiment adds **TRAIN-only annotated evidence questions**
alongside complete-notice training. Only the labelled predicate is projected;
other facts are not invented for a sentence. Contradictory standalone labels are
rejected. Source IDs, offsets, origins, hashes, unique passages and repeated
question-item counts are retained. Validation/calibration/test quotes cannot
enter this transformation.

Candidate `reliable-voting-evidence-matched-v2` starts from the immutable balanced
parent, freezes its encoder and performs two bounded head-training epochs. It
will be compared with the unchanged parent on original and fresh VALIDATION;
the losing model is retained. The faster scoped locator is also compared, rather
than assuming sentence scoring is necessarily better. No frozen/challenge label
selects weights, prompts, inference strategy or thresholds.
